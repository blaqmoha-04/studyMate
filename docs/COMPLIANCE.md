# Compliance audit

**This is an engineering audit of the software, not legal advice.** Passing every item below does not make an operator legally compliant: that depends on who runs StudyMate, where, and for whom. Templates for the policies are included; have a lawyer in your jurisdiction review them before the public uses the service. Items marked **YOU** can only be done by the operator.

Audit date: 2026-10-25 · Policy version in the app: `2026-10-25` · Automated checks: `npm test` (201 tests, includes the accessibility, consent and client-safety suites).

## Things only the operator can do (YOU)
1. **Register as a data controller with the Ghana Data Protection Commission** if you process personal data of people in Ghana. The Commission states registration is required before processing, that processing without it is an offence (Data Protection Act, 2012, Act 843, sections 53 and 56), and registration lasts two years then must be renewed. Source: dataprotection.org.gh/registration and DLA Piper's Ghana summary. If you are elsewhere, find your own regulator's equivalent.
2. **Fill in the operator details** (`OPERATOR_NAME`, `OPERATOR_ADDRESS`, `OPERATOR_EMAIL`). The policy pages show a red warning until you do and `deploy/scripts/preflight.sh` fails.
3. **Decide who may use the installation.** Sign-up has no age gate, age verification, or parental-consent flow. See item 16.
4. **Lawyer review** of the four policy pages, especially the limitation-of-liability, governing-law and data-retention wording.
5. **List outside providers** (hosting company, email provider) in `OPERATOR_PROCESSORS`, and make sure you have the contracts/terms with them that your law requires.
6. **Prepare a breach procedure** (who you notify and how fast). The Privacy Policy promises to tell affected people and the authority where the law requires.
7. **Manual accessibility testing** (keyboard-only pass, a screen reader, 200 % zoom, a phone) — see item 14.

## Known gaps (honest list)
- No age gate, age verification, or parental consent (item 16); operators must decide who may use their installation.
- No in-app prompt asking existing users to re-accept updated terms.
- No automated data-retention job for web-server (Nginx) logs.
- No data-protection impact assessment (the Ghana regulator asks for one in some cases) and no records of processing document; templates for these are outside this project.
- Accessibility has been checked by automated rules only; no assistive-technology testing has been done.
- Reference screenshot: the Settings page follows its structure (grey footer Save bar, Learning goal, Avatar initial, Danger Zone with outlined buttons) but it has not been compared visually side by side, and it uses the app's existing blue accent instead of the screenshot's teal. To switch to teal, change `--primary`, `--primary-hover` and `--primary-soft` at the top of `client/css/styles.css`, then re-run `npm test` (the contrast tests will tell you if your colours are too light).
